## Short description

BriefVu is an intake assistant for custom apparel shops. Staff paste a customer enquiry, review each extracted detail against the original evidence, resolve missing or conflicting information, and copy a focused clarification message or structured job spec.

## Medium description

BriefVu helps small screen-printing and embroidery shops turn incomplete customer emails, contact-form messages and DMs into reviewable job details. It extracts quantity, garments, decoration, artwork, timing and fulfilment information, keeps the customer’s evidence beside each value, and surfaces missing or conflicting details. Staff approve every change before copying a clarification draft or quote-ready job spec. BriefVu does not calculate the price or contact customers automatically; it prepares cleaner inputs for the shop’s existing quote workflow.

## Long description

Custom apparel enquiries often arrive as incomplete emails, contact-form messages or DMs. Quantity may be approximate, the garment style may be missing, print locations may be unclear, and the deadline may change in a later reply. BriefVu organizes that intake work before it reaches the quote desk.

The shop pastes an enquiry and chooses a ballpark or formal-quote workflow. BriefVu extracts the stated order details, links each result to the customer’s exact words, and separates confirmed, approximate, ambiguous, conflicting and missing information. Staff remain responsible for accepting, editing or removing every value. The product then drafts a consolidated clarification message containing only the unresolved questions.

When the customer replies, BriefVu proposes updates without silently replacing confirmed information. Once the required details are reviewed, the shop can copy a structured job spec into Printavo, YoPrint, a spreadsheet or its existing operating process.

The current version is designed for single-product custom apparel enquiries across common garments and decoration methods, including screen printing, embroidery, DTF and heat transfer. It does not generate final prices, inspect artwork files, schedule production or send messages automatically.

## Key capabilities

- Extract structured order details from informal customer messages.
- Keep exact source evidence beside each extracted value.
- Separate confirmed, approximate, ambiguous, conflicting and missing details.
- Generate one focused clarification draft for unresolved questions.
- Merge later customer replies through a human-approved review step.
- Recalculate readiness for a ballpark estimate or formal quote.
- Copy a plain-text or JSON job spec into the existing shop workflow.

## Safety and product boundary

- Human approval is required for every final value and outgoing message.
- BriefVu does not automatically quote, price or contact the customer.
- It does not claim that an attachment was received or artwork is production-ready without a human check.
- V0 is intended for one primary decorated apparel product per enquiry.
- Mixed-product bundles, personalization rosters and complex production matrices require manual handling.
