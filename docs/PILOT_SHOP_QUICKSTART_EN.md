# Briefvu Guided Pilot — Shop Quick Start

Briefvu helps your shop turn a customer enquiry into reviewable job details, a focused clarification message, and a structured job brief.

It does **not** calculate prices, send messages, check inventory, or approve production automatically. You remain responsible for every correction and customer reply.

## Best enquiries for the pilot

Start with one product per enquiry, such as:

- T-shirts or hoodies with screen printing or DTF;
- polos, caps, jackets, or workwear with embroidery;
- tote bags or safety garments with one decoration workflow.

For the first two weeks, handle these manually instead:

- multiple products in one enquiry;
- individual names, numbers, or player rosters;
- complex design × location × color matrices;
- orders that require attachment inspection;
- orders where you expect Briefvu to calculate the final price.

## The workflow

### 1. Paste the customer enquiry

Choose the quote mode:

- **Ballpark estimate** when approximate details are acceptable;
- **Formal quote** when price-affecting details must be resolved.

Remove customer-identifying details when they are not needed for the job.

### 2. Review every extracted field

- **Confirmed** means the customer stated one clear value.
- **Check it** means the value is approximate, ambiguous, conflicting, or needs review.
- **Missing** means the enquiry did not provide it.

Use the evidence below each field to compare the result with the customer’s exact words. Accept, edit, or remove anything before relying on it.

### 3. Resolve conflicts

If two values appear, Briefvu will not choose automatically. Select the correct candidate only when you have enough information. Otherwise leave it unresolved and ask the customer.

### 4. Copy the clarification draft

The draft asks only for unresolved details. Edit it in your normal email or messaging tool and send it yourself. Briefvu does not contact the customer.

### 5. Add the customer reply

Paste the reply into **Add customer reply**. Review each proposed change:

- **Accept** applies the proposal;
- **Edit** lets you correct it first;
- **Ignore** keeps the existing value.

Nothing should replace a confirmed value until you approve it.

### 6. Copy the Job Spec

Use **Copy text**, **Copy draft**, or **JSON** as appropriate.

- `QUOTE-READY JOB SPEC` means the selected quote mode has no remaining blocker.
- `DRAFT JOB SPEC` still has unresolved fields.
- `MANUAL REVIEW REQUIRED` means the order exceeds the current Pilot workflow.

Always perform your normal shop review before quoting or producing.

## When to stop and contact the Pilot team

Stop using the affected Job and report it immediately if:

- Briefvu says quote-ready when an essential fact is unresolved;
- a confirmed value changes without your approval;
- a multi-product or personalized order is treated as a normal single Job;
- copied output omits a visible conflict or manual-review warning;
- you see any customer content stored or displayed where it should not be.

Please record the time and action that caused the issue, but do not email customer-sensitive content unless the Pilot team requests a secure method.

## What feedback is most useful

- fields you had to correct;
- questions you removed or rewrote;
- orders you chose not to process in Briefvu;
- whether the Job Spec was actually used by your quote desk;
- approximate time saved or added;
- anything that reduced your trust.
